# Converting Estimates to Person-Months

A person-month is one person working full-time on the initiative for one calendar month, including normal meetings and breaks.

## Conversion Rules

| Input | Multiplier |
|-------|------------|
| Full-time, dedicated | 1× duration |
| Half-time | 0.5× duration |
| Two engineers in parallel | 2× duration |
| Story points | velocity ÷ 4 weeks per month |

## What Counts

By default, person-months include every role required to ship: design, engineering, QA, and rollout. Whatever the team agrees, apply uniformly across all initiatives in the same scoring round.

## What Does Not

- Discovery and research that would happen regardless
- Time spent only if the initiative wins prioritization
- Slack time that is not initiative-specific

If discovery is genuinely required for one initiative and not another, count it—just be consistent.

## Sanity Checks

- A 0.25 person-month estimate usually means "a few days." Decide whether that level of precision is worth carrying.
- A 12+ person-month estimate is a project, not an initiative. Break it down before scoring.
