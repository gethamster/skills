# Examples: Activating Customer Advocacy and Referral Programs

## Timing referral asks to a moment of value

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an online accounting service sent a referral email to every customer once a quarter. Few customers acted, and some replied with complaints.

**Walkthrough:**

The team replaces the quarterly blast with a trigger. When a customer files their first quarterly return through the service without contacting support, they receive a short note offering a referral link, with a credit for both them and the person they refer. Customers with open tickets are excluded.

The pilot runs with a small cohort first. The team tracks how many referred signups file their own first return, which tells them whether the program brings in customers who stay. The complaint replies stop because unhappy customers are no longer asked.

## Building a reference program for a long sales cycle

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a B2B security vendor loses deals at the final stage because it cannot supply references quickly. The same few customers get every request.

**Walkthrough:**

Customer success builds a reference list from accounts that renewed on time and scored well in the last satisfaction survey. Each account manager asks personally, explains that a call takes about half an hour and offers to schedule around the customer. A shared log records every request.

Sales now requests references through the log, which spreads calls across the list and shows who has been asked recently. The customers who carried every request before get a break.

## Starting a review program on a comparison site

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a small collaboration tool has few reviews on the comparison site its buyers read during consideration, and the existing ones are old.

**Walkthrough:**

The team adds a review request to the in-product message shown after a customer completes their first shared project. The request links straight to the review form and asks for an honest review, positive or negative. Support is asked to reply to every new review within a working day.

Over the following months the review count grows and the most recent reviews reflect the current product. Sales starts linking to the review page in consideration emails.
