# Examples: OST Workshop Facilitation

## A first tree with a new trio

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a newly formed trio at a pet-care marketplace has an agreed outcome, "more pet owners book a second sitter within two months," and has finished four story-based interviews. The product manager books a working session to build the first tree.

**Walkthrough:**

The day before, she shares the four interview snapshots and a short explainer of the tree's layers. The session opens with the outcome on the board. Everyone reads the snapshots again, then silently writes opportunities in the owner's voice and places them under the moments of a rough experience map: finding a sitter, preparing for the booking, during the stay, and after.

The group merges duplicates and reframes "I want sitter video calls" as "I worry about my dog when I'm away." It picks "During the stay" as the top branch, descends to "I don't know if my pet is eating," generates ideas silently, and chooses three. The session ends with an owner for each assumption test and a date for the next tree update. The branches it did not reach stay rough, and that is fine.

## A stakeholder walkthrough

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: the same trio meets the head of operations, who wants an in-app insurance offer, and the marketing lead.

**Walkthrough:**

The product manager starts with the outcome and asks whether anything has changed. She shows the top-level opportunities and asks what the team missed. The head of operations adds "I'm not sure what happens if something goes wrong," which she has heard from customer support. The team captures it and plans to listen for it in interviews.

When the walkthrough reaches solutions, the insurance idea is placed under the new opportunity instead of being argued against. Both stakeholders agree on what evidence would show that the new opportunity matters. The meeting ends with stakeholders contributing to the tree rather than debating its conclusions.

## A practice workshop before real interviews

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a product group at an insurance company wants to adopt opportunity solution trees, but no team has interview data yet.

**Walkthrough:**

A product lead prepares a fictional case with a few short interview summaries and a simple dashboard. Small groups build trees from the case, compare their structures, and discuss where they disagreed. The lead is clear that this is practice. Afterward, each team commits to running its first real interviews before building a tree for its own outcome.
