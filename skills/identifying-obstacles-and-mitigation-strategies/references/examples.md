# Examples: Identifying V2MOM Obstacles and Mitigation Strategies

## A premortem uncovers a hidden dependency

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a product team's V2MOM has "launch usage-based pricing" as its top method. The first draft of the obstacles section says only "engineering capacity."

**Walkthrough:**

The lead runs a premortem and asks everyone to write why the launch failed. Several people independently write that billing changes depend on the finance systems team, whose own V2MOM does not mention pricing at all. The obstacle is rewritten as "The finance systems team has not planned the billing changes the pricing launch needs." It goes to the top of the list, the product lead owns the response, and the first action is to raise the gap with the owner of the company V2MOM, who adds the billing work to the finance systems team's methods.

## An individual names personal obstacles honestly

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a new engineering manager writes a personal V2MOM with the method "run effective one-on-ones with every report." The obstacles section is empty.

**Walkthrough:**

Their manager asks what would make the method fail. The new manager admits they keep cancelling one-on-ones when incidents happen and that they have never given difficult feedback. Those become two obstacles: "one-on-ones get cancelled during incidents" and "no experience giving critical feedback." The responses are to protect a fixed slot that incidents do not override without a replacement, and to shadow a senior manager's feedback conversation in the first month. At the next review, both obstacles are checked first.

## Obstacles read side by side reveal a conflict

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: at a cross-team alignment review, the sales team lists "product is not ready for the enterprise launch" as an obstacle, while the product team lists "sales has promised features we have not scoped."

**Walkthrough:**

Read separately, each obstacle looks like the other team's problem. Read together, they describe one conflict about scope and timing. The facilitator assigns a joint owner from each team and a single response: agree on a written enterprise scope within two weeks and have the company V2MOM owner confirm it. Both teams replace their original obstacle with a reference to the agreed scope.
