# FAQ: Mapping Current Reality Using the Six Pillars Framework

## How long should a current reality audit take for a site with fewer than 100 pages?

For a small site, expect 2-3 hours for a thorough first audit. The content inventory and technical crawl complete quickly because the dataset is small. The time-consuming parts, competitive analysis and audience search behavior, remain roughly constant regardless of site size because you are analyzing external data. Subsequent quarterly audits typically take 60-90 minutes because you are updating an existing document rather than building from scratch.

## Should I complete the current reality audit before or after building topical authority clusters?

Always complete the current reality audit first. The audit reveals which topic clusters you already cover, how deeply you cover them, and where gaps exist. Without that baseline, your cluster architecture is based on assumptions rather than evidence. The Current Reality Document directly feeds the [topical authority building](https://tryhamster.com/skills/building-topical-authority-through-content-clustering) and [semantic keyword mapping](https://tryhamster.com/skills/creating-semantic-keyword-maps) skills.

Starting with clusters before auditing often leads to duplicating content you already have or ignoring content that could be repurposed.

## How do I map current reality if I have no access to paid SEO tools?

Google Search Console provides content performance data (impressions, clicks, position, CTR) at no cost. Screaming Frog's free tier crawls up to 500 URLs. Google Trends provides directional search demand data. For competitive analysis without paid tools, use manual SERP checks for your top 20 keywords, noting which domains appear and what SERP features are present.

The audit will have less precise competitive metrics (you will not know competitor Domain Rating, for instance), so note that limitation in your Data Gaps section. The audit is still highly valuable because your own Search Console data is the most accurate performance data available for your domain.

## What do I do if the audit reveals that my strongest content cluster is also my most competitive?

This is common and not necessarily a problem. Strong competition in your best cluster means there is significant search demand worth pursuing. The key question is whether your current position is defensible. If you hold positions 1-5 for core keywords in that cluster, the audit validates continued investment.

If you hold positions 10-20 despite significant content, the audit may reveal that competitors have structural advantages (more backlinks, better internal linking, fresher content) that you need to address before expanding to new clusters.

## Why does my Current Reality Document keep drifting from facts into recommendations?

This happens because SEO practitioners are trained to solve problems, so identifying a problem and immediately prescribing a fix feels natural. The fix is structural: create the document template with clearly labeled sections and enforce a rule that only the 'Quick Wins' section contains actions. For everything else, write observations in the format 'We observed [metric] which indicates [interpretation]' rather than 'We should [action].' If a recommendation surfaces while writing, capture it in a separate scratch document and evaluate it during the strategy phase.

## How does the current reality audit differ from a standard SEO audit?

A standard SEO audit focuses on finding and fixing technical and on-page issues. The current reality audit includes technical health but adds four dimensions that typical audits skip: topical authority measurement, competitive positioning analysis, audience search behavior trends, and link equity distribution. The purpose is also different. A standard audit produces a fix-it list.

The current reality audit produces a strategic baseline that informs futures thinking, scenario planning, and [content timing decisions](https://tryhamster.com/skills/timing-content-interventions-strategically). Think of the standard audit as a mechanic's inspection and the current reality audit as a navigator's chart.

## Can I automate parts of the current reality audit for quarterly repetition?

Yes, and you should. The data collection steps (Search Console export, crawl execution, backlink profile pull) can be scripted or scheduled. Many SEO platforms allow saved reports that auto-update. What cannot be automated is the qualitative interpretation: reading SERP screenshots, identifying competitive strategy shifts, and writing the executive summary.

A good approach is to automate data collection into a standardized spreadsheet template, then spend your human time on interpretation and synthesis. For teams using AI agents, the Current Reality Document format is designed to be parseable as context for downstream tasks.
