# Examples: Timing Content Interventions for SEO Pillars

## A seasonal topic published at the peak

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a tax software company publishes its guide to year-end tax preparation each year in the same week that demand peaks. The page ranks, but late.

**Walkthrough:**

The timing note shows demand starting to climb about six weeks before the peak, using Google Trends for the shape and Search Console for the company's own queries. The team's process from draft to indexed page takes about four weeks.

The calendar now schedules a substantive refresh of the guide to start ten weeks before the peak, with new rules and examples added each year. The page's date changes only when the content does. The team checks after the season whether the page gained impressions earlier than the year before.

## A declining topic with too many pages

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a marketing blog has many posts about a social platform feature that its audience has mostly stopped using. Search Console shows impressions for all of them falling along with demand.

**Walkthrough:**

The timing note classifies the topic as declining. The team keeps one page with the best remaining information, merges the useful parts of the others into it, and redirects the rest. The saved time goes to a rising topic inside the blog's chosen pillars.

The consolidated page is set to leave-alone status, with a trigger to revisit it if demand turns up again.

## A topic that swings between two framings

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a workplace software company covers remote work. Over several years, the language its audience uses swings between remote work and office return, and interest in each framing rises and falls.

**Walkthrough:**

The team classifies the topic as a pendulum and restructures the pillar page to cover both framings, with cluster pages for each. The calendar schedules a review each time the balance of interest shifts, using Google Trends to compare the two framings.

When the balance turns, the team refreshes the cluster pages for the rising framing with new material and updates the pillar page's emphasis. It does not create a new page for each swing.
