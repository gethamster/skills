# FAQ: Six Pillar SEO Strategy: Implementing End to End

## What is a six pillar SEO strategy?

It is an SEO and content plan built by running Sohail Inayatullah's six pillars of futures thinking in sequence: mapping, anticipating, timing, deepening, creating alternatives and transforming. Each pillar produces a short document, and the last one turns the rest into a roadmap of pillar pages, clusters and publishing order.

## Is this the same as lists of SEO pillars such as technical, on-page and off-page?

No. Those lists divide SEO into areas of work. The six pillars here are stages of strategic thinking taken from futures studies. Technical, on-page and link work still happen, and they show up in the current-reality map and in the roadmap the backcast produces.

## How long does a full pass take?

It depends on the size of the site and how many people take part. A small team can run a light version in a few weeks of part-time work. A larger organization with workshops for each pillar will take longer. Quarterly reviews afterward are much shorter.

## Can we skip pillars?

You can focus on some pillars when time is short, and Inayatullah describes selecting a single pillar as a valid way to use the framework. For a strategy meant to guide a year or more, try to produce at least a light output for every pillar, because each one catches a different kind of mistake.

## Who should be involved?

The content or SEO lead owns the program. Include product, sales or customer-facing staff for mapping and deepening, and someone who can approve the preferred position. Customers or outsiders are especially useful in the deepening session.
