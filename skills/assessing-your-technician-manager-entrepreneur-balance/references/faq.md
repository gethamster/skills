# FAQ: E-Myth Self-Assessment

## Is there an official E-Myth quiz?

This skill does not reproduce one. Its quiz statements are written from published descriptions of the three roles and are meant as a check on your time log. The time log is the main evidence; the quiz and ratings help explain why your time goes where it does.

## How long does the assessment take?

Expect about two hours the first time, most of it spent gathering and tagging activities. Later reassessments are faster because you are updating an existing scorecard. If you have no records, add a week of tracking before you start.

## What if my result says I am mostly a Technician?

That is the most common result for owners, and Gerber built his framework around it. Whether it is a problem depends on your stage and goals. A solo practitioner may be right where they should be, while a founder trying to grow a team needs to shift time toward the other roles.

## Should co-founders do the assessment together?

Do it separately first, so each person records honestly, then combine the scorecards. The combined view shows whether the team covers all three roles and where roles overlap. Assign the team-level gap to a specific person.

## How often should I repeat it?

Quarterly works for most businesses, plus after big changes such as a key hire or a new line of business. Compare each scorecard with the last to see whether the gap you worked on is closing.
