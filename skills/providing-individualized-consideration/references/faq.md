# FAQ: Providing Individualized Consideration and Coaching

## What is individualized consideration in transformational leadership?

It is the component focused on attending to each follower's needs, concerns, development and growth rather than managing everyone identically. In practice it shows up as mentoring, coaching, support, open communication and tailored growth opportunities. It sits alongside the other three components described on the method page.

## How is individualized consideration different from favoritism?

Favoritism gives some people better outcomes or lower standards. Individualized consideration keeps the same standards and fairness for everyone while changing the route: how feedback is framed, which development fits, and how recognition is delivered. If one person is getting easier expectations, that is not tailoring, it is a double standard.

## How do I learn what motivates each team member?

Ask directly and listen in regular one-on-ones, then observe what energizes and drains them in real work. Record motivations, strengths, weaknesses, aspirations and development needs, and mark which are confirmed by the person versus your assumptions. Revisit the profile after role changes or setbacks, since motivations shift.

## What are the benefits of transformational leadership when coaching individuals?

Coaching fitted to the person supports growth they recognize as their own, rather than generic development they tolerate. Reliable follow-through on commitments builds trust, which makes feedback easier to hear. Individual recognition also tells people their specific contribution was seen, which general team praise does not.

## Can I practice individualized consideration with a large team?

Yes, but you need lightweight systems. Keep a brief profile and a commitment list per person, and protect regular one-on-one time even if it is short. If the team is too large for any individual contact, delegate coaching to leads and apply the same practices to your relationship with them.
