# FAQ: Facilitating V2MOM Planning Sessions

## Who should facilitate a V2MOM session?

Ideally someone other than the V2MOM owner, such as a peer leader, a chief of staff or an outside facilitator. That frees the owner to participate and make final calls without also running the room. If the owner must facilitate, they should state the decision rule at the start and share their own views last.

## How long should a V2MOM workshop be?

A company or department V2MOM usually needs a half day to a full day for a first draft, often split into two sessions. Values and methods rankings take the most time. Team and individual V2MOMs can often be drafted in a shorter session once the parent document exists.

## What if the group cannot agree on a ranking?

Name the disagreement clearly, including what each side believes and which scenario divides them. Then let the V2MOM owner decide and record the reasoning. A decision the group understands is better than a tie that leaves the conflict unresolved until the next crisis.

## How do you run a V2MOM session remotely?

Use one shared document for the template, breakout rooms for pairs and small groups, and silent writing in the document or a digital board. Keep timeboxes tighter than in person, since attention drops faster on video. Send the draft out soon after the call so people who missed parts of the discussion can catch up.

## Should the whole V2MOM be finished in one session?

The session should produce an agreed draft, not a polished final document. Wording, precise targets and some open questions are better handled afterward by owners with the right data. Set a date for the final version before the session ends.
