# Examples: Facilitating V2MOM Planning Sessions

## A leadership offsite that almost stalled on the vision

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a leadership team of six meets for a half-day session to draft the company V2MOM. After the first hour, they are still debating two words in the vision.

**Walkthrough:**

The facilitator pauses the discussion and asks each person to write, silently, what the company will have achieved by year end. Five of the six answers describe the same destination in different words. The facilitator reads the shared elements aloud, the group confirms the substance, and the CEO takes the wording as a follow-up. The session moves to values with most of its time left, and the ranking discussion, which is where the real disagreement was, gets the attention it needed.

## A department session where quiet people change the ranking

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a product department of ten runs its V2MOM workshop over a video call. The department head has a clear view that "new features" should be the top method.

**Walkthrough:**

The facilitator asks the head to hold their view until the end and runs a 1-2-4-All round: silent writing, then pairs in breakout rooms, then groups of four, then the whole call. Several groups independently raise the same concern: customers are churning over reliability issues that no method addresses. When the head shares last, they agree the pattern is strong enough to put reliability first. The ranking changes, and the head notes it would not have heard the concern in an open discussion.

## A premortem surfaces an uncomfortable obstacle

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a sales leadership team's first pass at obstacles produces only "competition" and "budget."

**Walkthrough:**

The facilitator runs a premortem: "It is year end and we missed the plan badly. Write down why." Collected anonymously, several notes say the new compensation plan rewards closing small deals over the enterprise deals the V2MOM prioritizes. Nobody had said it out loud. The group adds it as the top obstacle, the head of sales takes ownership, and the response is to review the compensation plan with finance before the year starts.
