# Examples: Defining V2MOM Methods and Action Plans

## A marketing team cuts a long list to a ranked few

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a marketing team's first draft lists nine methods, from "post on social media daily" to "rebuild the website." The company V2MOM's top method is "win mid-sized accounts in two new industries."

**Walkthrough:**

The team rewrites each item by asking what it is for. Daily posting and a newsletter merge into "build an audience in the two target industries." Several items turn out to be business-as-usual work and move off the list. The owner test removes one item nobody would own. The team ends with four methods, ranked by how directly each serves the company's top method, and moves the website rebuild to the "not this period" list with a one-line reason. Each method gets an owner and three first actions.

## An engineer writes personal methods under a team V2MOM

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a backend engineer's team V2MOM has "make the billing service reliable enough that support stops escalating billing bugs" as its first method. The engineer drafts personal methods that include "learn a new language" and "fix bugs."

**Walkthrough:**

The engineer's manager asks which team method each item serves. "Fix bugs" becomes "own the retry and idempotency work in the billing service," which points at the team's first method and has a clear first action: review the recent billing escalations. "Learn a new language" is kept as a lower-ranked growth method because the team's values include developing people, and the engineer ties it to a small internal tool. The final list has three ranked methods, each linked to a team method or value.

## A leadership team resolves a resourcing conflict with the ranking

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: halfway through the year, a platform team is asked to support both an enterprise security review and a new integrations launch. Both are methods in the company V2MOM.

**Walkthrough:**

Instead of escalating, the platform lead checks the company V2MOM. The security work is the second method and integrations is the fourth, and the top value is customer trust. The lead staffs the security review first and tells the integrations owner, pointing to the ranking. The integrations owner agrees to a later date, and the next review records the slip as a known consequence of the ranking rather than a surprise.
