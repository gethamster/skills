# FAQ: Facilitating Impact Mapping Workshops

## Who should attend an impact mapping workshop?

It depends on the purpose. The impactmapping.org facilitation guide says vision workshops should involve all the key stakeholders, with delivery team members joining a second workshop later to propose deliverables. For focusing delivery, stakeholders and the delivery team should build the map together. For reframing a problem, one facilitator meets stakeholders in smaller groups instead of holding a single workshop.

## How long should the workshop be?

Long enough for the depth you need. Justin Holmes, writing on Opensource.com, estimates about half an hour to reverse engineer a simple map for a current project, and around half a day for a prepared facilitator to reach a map with basic metrics. Agreeing the goal in advance, as the facilitation guide recommends for delivery work, shortens the main session.

## Can impact mapping be run remotely?

Yes, with a shared digital board in place of a wall. Keep the same structure: goal visible, one level at a time, silent writing before discussion. Remote sessions often work better split into shorter blocks, for example one for goal and actors and another for impacts and priorities.

## What should the facilitator do when stakeholders keep proposing features?

Write each feature down on a parking area and ask which behavior it is meant to change. Put that behavior on the map as an impact and keep the feature as a candidate deliverable. Mashooq Badar describes this approach in his Codurance write-up, where participants kept proposing features until asked what behavior each one supported.

## What happens to the map after the workshop?

For vision work, the facilitation guide says facilitators often turn the map into another document, such as a vision paper or a request for proposals, and keep the conclusions. For focusing delivery, teams usually keep the map in its original form and review metrics against it during delivery. Either way, send out the map, decisions and parked questions promptly.
