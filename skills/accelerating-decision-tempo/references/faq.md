# FAQ: Accelerating Decision Tempo Under Uncertainty

## Does a faster OODA Loop always mean better decisions?

No. The goal is tempo relative to the environment, and one guide defines it as [completing cycles faster than the surrounding situation changes](https://goalsandprogress.com/ooda-loop-personal-decisions-master-rapid-decision-cycles). Beyond that pace, extra speed adds churn and reversals without new information. For the broader argument about tempo versus raw speed, see the [OODA Loop method page](https://tryhamster.com/methods/ooda-loop).

## How long should I spend in the Orient phase?

There is no universal limit. A practitioner guide suggests time-boxing orientation to the stakes, with examples of [30 minutes, an hour or a day](https://goalsandprogress.com/ooda-loop-personal-decisions-master-rapid-decision-cycles). Start with a box that fits how costly and reversible the decision is, then adjust it based on how often your reviews show you needed more or less time.

## How is this different from just making snap decisions?

Snap decisions skip the test and the review. This skill frames each decision as a [hypothesis with a disconfirming signal](https://modelthinkers.com/mental-model/ooda-loop), checks the result at a set time and feeds it into the next cycle. It also calibrates intuition through [pattern-recognition review](https://hiperformanceculture.com/decisions/ooda-loop/decisions-ooda-loop-guide), which snap judgment never does.

## What should I do when the time box ends and I still feel unsure?

Commit to the best option on current information unless something material changed during orientation. Feeling unsure is normal because uncertainty rarely disappears in a changing situation. Make the action as small and reversible as possible, name the signal that would reverse it, and let the result inform the next cycle.

## How often should I review past decisions for patterns?

Pick a regular cadence that fits your decision volume, for example weekly for operational calls or quarterly for strategic ones. The point is to look back at [patterns you recognized or missed](https://hiperformanceculture.com/decisions/ooda-loop/decisions-ooda-loop-guide) while details are still fresh. Short, regular reviews tend to stay honest, while rare long ones get skipped.
