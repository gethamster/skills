# FAQ: Defining PM Competency Levels from Associate to Senior

## Does Cabage publish a leveling matrix?

No. His page describes where junior and senior PMs tend to work on the chart, with juniors at the bottom and seniors in the upper part, but it does not assign boxes to levels or define a rating scale. The matrix is something each company writes on top of his grid.

## How many levels should the matrix cover?

Use the levels your company already has. The grid works with any number, because each level simply sits a little higher on the chart than the one below. If you have separate management levels, keep them on a separate track.

## Should senior PMs still be good at tactical work?

Yes. Senior levels usually keep the tactical boxes as a foundation, marked as owned or contributed, while their own time shifts toward strategic boxes. A senior PM who cannot write clear requirements makes it harder for the team to deliver.

## How do we handle different product types?

Write more than one path. Cabage places technical PMs on internal tools and platforms on the right side of the chart and consumer PMs on the left, so senior expectations should differ by side. Keep level names and pay the same across paths.

## What counts as evidence for promotion?

Sustained work in the next level's owned boxes, shown through several real examples that meet the written expectations. One strong project can be a starting point for the conversation. A pattern over time is what the criteria should require.
