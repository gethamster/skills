# FAQ: Choosing Kanban Software

## What are the best kanban board tools?

The best tool is the one that supports the practices your team runs and fits the systems you already use. A tool is a good fit for kanban if it lets columns match your real workflow, shows WIP limits, displays policies and records when items move between columns. Start from those needs and test a short list on real work.

## Should we choose a tool before or after designing the board?

After, if you can. Designing the board first, even on a whiteboard, tells you what the tool must support. Choosing a tool first tends to let its default template shape your workflow.

## Can a free tool support a serious kanban practice?

Often, yes, for a single team. Check that the free tier includes column limits, the history needed for flow metrics and the integrations you need. If metrics are missing, column timestamps plus a spreadsheet can fill the gap.

## How long should the trial be?

Long enough to run your normal cadences on real work, usually a couple of weeks. A short demo hides problems that only appear with your own items and people. End the trial with a scoring session while the experience is fresh.

## How do we handle resistance to switching tools?

Involve the people who use the board daily in choosing the criteria and in the trial. Explain what the new tool fixes and what it costs them in effort. Migrate active work only, keep the old board read-only for a while, and review the setup together after a few weeks.
