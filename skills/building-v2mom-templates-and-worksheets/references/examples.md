# Examples: Building V2MOM Templates and Worksheets

## A one-page template for team V2MOMs

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an operations lead at a company of about two hundred people is preparing the first cascade and needs a single template for department and team V2MOMs.

**Walkthrough:**

The lead builds a shared document with a header block (owner, level, period, parent V2MOM link, last updated) and five headings in order. Values and methods are numbered lists with the note "order is priority." Each method line has two short fields: parent method code and owner. Measures are a small table with metric, baseline, target, date, owner and parent measure. Obstacles have a column for the method they threaten. The lead fills in an invented example for a fictional support team, publishes both side by side, and asks three team leads to try it before the cascade opens.

## Testing the template exposes a confusing prompt

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: during testing, two of three volunteer authors leave the obstacles section almost empty.

**Walkthrough:**

The lead asks why. Both authors say the prompt, "List risks," made them think of a formal risk register, which felt out of scope. The lead rewrites the prompt as "What would make each of your top methods fail? Name the method." and adds the method column. In the next round, all three authors list specific obstacles tied to methods. The change is recorded in the template's version note.

## A shorter version for individuals

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: engineers find the team template too heavy for personal V2MOMs and start writing free-form notes instead.

**Walkthrough:**

The lead creates a short individual version with the same five headings and the same order, but fewer fields: a one-line vision, the top three values inherited from the team with room to restate them, up to three methods each with a team method code, a short obstacles list and a measures table with only metric, target and date. Because the headings match, managers can still read individual documents against the team V2MOM. Free-form notes stop appearing within the next cycle.
