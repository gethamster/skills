# FAQ: Migrating From Flat to Usage-Based AI Pricing

## How long does a migration take?

Plan for several months: time to build and verify metering, a period of new-customer-only pricing, at least one cycle of shadow billing, and waves for existing customers. Annual contracts extend the tail, since they usually move at renewal. Rushing the early steps is what causes the public failures.

## Should I redesign tiers before migrating?

Yes. The migration moves customers onto a tier design, so that design needs to exist and to clear the margin floor first. Test it on new customers before existing ones move.

## How much churn should I expect?

It depends on how many customers see a higher bill and how large the increase is. The impact model tells you which accounts are at risk before you announce anything. Plan retention offers for that group and measure churn per cohort against the plan.

## How do I handle enterprise customers on multi-year contracts?

Leave the contract terms in place until renewal unless the customer asks to move early. Use the time to show them their usage and a preview of the new pricing, so renewal is a planned conversation. Offer committed volume with a published overage rate, which gives procurement the predictability it needs.

## Can I move only the AI features to usage pricing?

Yes, and it is often the least disruptive path. Keep the core product on its existing plan and add a usage component or add-on for the AI features whose cost varies. Customers who use little AI see little change.
