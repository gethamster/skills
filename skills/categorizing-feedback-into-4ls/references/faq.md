# FAQ: Sorting Team Feedback into the 4Ls Categories

## What is the simplest way to tell the categories apart?

Ask what the team should do with the note. Keep doing it means Liked. Write it down or teach it means Learned. Fix the gap means Lacked. Propose or request something means Longed For.

## Does it matter if a note is in the wrong column?

Less than people think. The categories are prompts that help people think of different kinds of feedback. Sorting is worth fixing only where it changes how clusters and votes reflect what people meant. Move a note only when its position would split a theme or confuse the vote.

## Who decides where a note goes?

The writer. They know what they meant. The facilitator can suggest a move, but should ask first rather than rearranging the board alone.

## Should duplicate notes be removed?

No. Group them and keep every note visible inside the group. The number of people who raised the same thing is useful information for the vote.

## What about a note that fits no category?

It is often a question, a request for information or a topic for another meeting. Put it in a parking area and make sure someone follows up on it.
