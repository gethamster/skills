# Examples: Writing 4Ls Retrospective Questions for Each Category

## Questions after an incident

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a sprint included a production incident that took a day to resolve. The facilitator wants questions that surface lessons without turning into blame.

**Walkthrough:**

The facilitator keeps one stable question per category and adds one tied to the incident. Liked: "What helped most while we were handling the incident?" Learned: "What do you know now about our system that you did not know before the incident?" Lacked: "What information or access was missing during the incident?" Longed For: "What would make the next incident easier to handle?"

Each question points at conditions, not people. The board fills with specific notes about missing runbooks and alerting, which lead to two clear actions.

## Questions for a quiet, steady sprint

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a sprint went smoothly and the facilitator worries the team will write very little.

**Walkthrough:**

The facilitator sharpens the questions toward small frictions. Liked: "What small habit made your week easier?" Learned: "What did you figure out that you would tell a new teammate?" Lacked: "Where did you wait on something, even briefly?" Longed For: "What one small change would you make to how we work?"

The team writes more notes than expected, mostly small ones, and picks one low-effort improvement to try.

## Questions for a new team

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team formed two sprints ago and is still learning how to work together. People are polite and cautious.

**Walkthrough:**

The facilitator keeps questions simple and close to the four words, and adds a line explaining each category. Liked: "What worked well for you this sprint?" Learned: "What did you learn about the product or the team?" Lacked: "What would have made your work easier?" Longed For: "What do you hope we try next sprint?"

Anonymous notes are turned on. The facilitator saves the questions that produced useful notes in a small library for later sprints.
