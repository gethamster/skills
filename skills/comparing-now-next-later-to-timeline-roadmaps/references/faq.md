# FAQ: Comparing Now-Next-Later to Quarterly and Timeline Roadmaps

## How do I handle stakeholders who insist on dates even when our assessment clearly favors Now-Next-Later?

Start by understanding what the stakeholder actually needs the dates for. Often they need coordination signals, not precise dates. ' For coordination signals, offer a hybrid where only high-certainty Now items receive approximate timeframes. For contractual date needs, maintain a separate timeline view derived from the Now-Next-Later board.

Address the underlying need rather than debating the format.

## How long should the transition from a quarterly roadmap to Now-Next-Later take?

Plan for four to eight weeks total. The first week covers assessment and recommendation. Weeks two and three cover item mapping and stakeholder communication. Weeks three through six are the parallel period where both formats coexist.

Weeks six through eight are the wind-down of the old format. Rushing the transition by skipping the parallel period creates stakeholder confusion and increases the chance of reverting back to the old format under pressure.

## Should I compare now next later vs quarterly roadmap formats every quarter, or only when problems arise?

Reassess quarterly, even when things seem fine. The reassessment takes only 30 minutes: re-score the five dimensions and compare to previous scores. Most quarters you will confirm the current format is still appropriate. But environments change gradually, and a quarterly check catches shifts before they become acute problems.

Waiting until something breaks means you have already been using the wrong format for months.

## Can I use Now-Next-Later for some teams and quarterly roadmaps for others within the same organization?

Yes, and this is more common than a single format for the whole organization. Different teams face different conditions. A compliance team with regulatory deadlines legitimately needs timeline roadmaps. A product innovation team exploring new markets legitimately needs Now-Next-Later.

The key is to document each team's format choice with its assessment scores, so the decision is transparent and revisitable. You also need a coordination mechanism when cross-team dependencies exist. Typically one team provides date estimates for the specific items that the timeline-format team depends on.

## What tools work best for maintaining a hybrid Now-Next-Later and timeline roadmap setup?

The tool matters less than the translation discipline. Any tool that supports both kanban-style boards and timeline views can work (Productboard, Jira, Linear, Notion, or even a spreadsheet with two tabs). The critical requirement is that the Now-Next-Later view is the source of truth and the timeline view is derived from it, not maintained independently. If your tool allows you to tag items as 'timeline-eligible' and filter to a timeline view, that reduces the manual translation burden significantly.

## How do I prevent the Now-Next-Later board from becoming an unstructured backlog?

This happens when teams skip graduation criteria and treat Later as a parking lot for every idea. Set explicit limits on each horizon: Now should hold three to five items per team, Next should hold five to eight items, and Later should hold no more than 10 to 15 items. Anything beyond those limits goes to a separate ideation backlog, not the roadmap. Review and prune each horizon regularly using the criteria described in [defining graduation criteria between horizons](https://tryhamster.com/skills/defining-graduation-criteria-between-horizons).

A roadmap is a communication tool, not a storage system.

## Why does my roadmap format keep drifting back to timeline-based even after we switched to Now-Next-Later?

Format drift usually happens for one of three reasons. ' and the team starts adding informal dates to avoid the conversation. Second, the team never fully retired the old format, so both coexist and the familiar one wins by default. Third, the team did not run the assessment properly and the environment actually favors a hybrid or timeline format.

Diagnose by checking which of these three causes applies. For the first cause, coach stakeholders on how to read the Now-Next-Later format. For the second, set a hard retirement date for the old format. For the third, re-run the assessment honestly.
