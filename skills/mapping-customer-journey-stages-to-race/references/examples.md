# Examples: RACE Funnel Mapping

## A software company decides where the free trial belongs

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a self-serve software company describes its journey as discover, sign up for trial, activate, upgrade, renew. Marketing counts trial sign-ups as conversions, while finance counts only paid upgrades.

**Walkthrough:**

The team writes RACE definitions for its business first. Act is any meaningful interaction, including a trial sign-up. Convert is a paid upgrade. The alignment matrix then places discover in Reach, sign-up and activation in Act, upgrade in Convert, and renewal in Engage.

The mapping exposes a broken transition: activated trial users receive nothing between activation and the trial's end. The team adds an in-product upgrade prompt and a sales follow-up for large accounts as Convert touchpoints, and marketing stops reporting trial sign-ups as conversions.

## A furniture retailer maps an online and in-store journey

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a furniture retailer knows that many customers browse online and then buy in a showroom. Its funnel reports treat the website as the only place a sale can happen.

**Walkthrough:**

The matrix places online browsing and saved lists in Act, and both online checkout and showroom purchases in Convert. Showroom appointments booked online become an Act touchpoint with its own KPI. Delivery, assembly support and review requests go into Engage.

Mapping touchpoints shows that the showroom team has no way to know which visitors came from the website. The action is to capture the appointment source at booking, so Convert can be measured across both channels.

## A professional services firm finds an Engage gap

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an accounting firm maps its journey for small-business clients: referral, first call, proposal, engagement, year-end.

**Walkthrough:**

Referral maps to Reach, the first call to Act, proposal and signed engagement to Convert, and the working relationship to Engage. The Engage row has one touchpoint: the year-end meeting.

The team adds two Engage touchpoints, a mid-year check-in and a short note when tax rules change, and marks referrals from existing clients as the loop back into Reach. Referral requests get an owner for the first time.
