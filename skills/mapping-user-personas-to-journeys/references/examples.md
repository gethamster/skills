# Examples: Story Map Personas: Mapping Personas to User Journeys

## A clinic booking system with three user types

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team is mapping an appointment system for a small clinic. The people involved are patients, reception staff and clinicians. The first draft of the map mixes all three in one line of story.

**Walkthrough:**

The team picks the patient as the primary persona, because the product fails if patients cannot book. They map the patient's journey first: find a time, book, get a reminder, check in, attend, pay. Reception staff enter the story at check-in and payment, so the team places their tasks there and draws vertical lines where the story changes hands. Clinicians enter only to view the day's list and write notes.

With the columns tagged by persona, the team sees that the patient and reception share check-in. The first release line covers the patient's journey and the reception tasks it depends on, while the clinician's notes stay in the existing system for now. The card to the left of the slice names patients and reception as the target users.

## Replacing a proto persona with research

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team mapping a budgeting app assumed its main user was a young professional tracking spending on a phone. A handful of interviews run after the first mapping session tell a different story.

**Walkthrough:**

Most interviewees are couples managing a shared household budget, and the key moment is agreeing on a monthly plan together. The team rewrites the persona and walks the backbone again. "Invite my partner" and "agree the plan" become early tasks, and the solo spending-tracker tasks move lower. The persona card on the map now says which parts came from interviews and which remain assumptions.

## Accounting for the chooser

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team building a scheduling tool for field technicians mapped the technicians' day in detail. Sales reports that deals stall because operations managers cannot see how the tool would be rolled out.

**Walkthrough:**

The team adds the operations manager as the chooser. Their tasks, such as trial the tool with one crew and import the team list, go at the start of the map, before the technicians' day begins. The first release now includes a basic import so a trial can start, which the technician-only map had missed.
