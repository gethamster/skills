# FAQ: SEO Scenario Planning for Alternative Futures

## What is SEO scenario planning?

It is building several plausible futures for how search and your market could change, testing your content plan against each, and sorting the plan into moves that work in every future and bets that wait for a signal. It is the creating alternatives pillar of the six pillars framework applied to search.

## How many scenarios should we build?

Four is common when crossing two uncertainties, and two or three can work for a small team. Fewer than two defeats the purpose. Many more become hard to discuss and use.

## Where do the uncertainties come from?

From the current-reality map, the emerging issues list and the deepening session. Good uncertainties are external to your team, would change your plan, and are genuinely open. Changes in how search results are presented, category growth and competitor moves are common choices.

## How is this different from a risk register?

A risk register lists things that could go wrong and how to respond. Scenarios describe whole alternative futures, including ones that are good for you, and test the entire plan against them. The two can work together: the worst-case scenario often feeds the risk register.

## What happens after the scenarios are built?

The sorted plan goes to the transforming pillar, where the team chooses a preferred future and backcasts from it. The indicators go into the quarterly review so bets start when their signals appear.
