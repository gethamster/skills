# FAQ: Assessing Product Team Strengths and Skill Gaps

## Does every PM need to cover every box?

No. Cabage states that the framework does not assume every PM should be able to do everything. Coverage is judged at the team level, and individual PMs are expected to specialize in the part of the chart their role needs.

## What rating scale should we use?

Cabage's page does not define one. A short scale with written meanings, such as not yet, capable and strong, works for most teams. If you want more detail, Cagan's approach rates both importance and ability for each skill so you can see the size of each gap.

## Should the assessment feed into performance reviews?

Keep it separate. Once ratings affect pay or promotion directly, self-ratings tend to inflate and the team chart stops showing real gaps. Use the assessment for development and hiring decisions, and use your normal review process for performance.

## How often should we reassess?

Reassess when the team or the product changes, such as after hires, departures, a reorganization or a new product line. Between those events, a light check at regular development conversations is usually enough. Keep each chart so you can compare them over time.

## What if the whole team is weak in one region?

A regional gap usually means the team works in one mode, and individual training rarely fixes it quickly. Consider hiring someone whose home quadrant is that region, and meanwhile give the region to one PM who wants to grow toward it.
