# Examples: Assessing Product Team Strengths and Skill Gaps

## A delivery-heavy team finds a strategic gap

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a B2B software company has five PMs who ship reliably, but leadership feels the roadmap is reactive. The head of product runs a team assessment on the grid.

**Walkthrough:**

Each PM and their manager rate the boxes marked core. The overlay shows Core Product and the tactical Market boxes well covered by several PMs. The strategic boxes under Users and Market are grey: nobody is doing regular jobs-to-be-done work, market positioning or competitive gap analysis.

This matches the regional gap in Cabage's own example. Because it is a whole region, the head of product decides to hire a senior PM whose home quadrant is upper-left, and asks one existing PM who wants to grow in that direction to own competitive gap analysis in the meantime.

## A single owner hides a risk

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a platform team of three PMs looks well covered at first glance. One PM is about to go on extended leave.

**Walkthrough:**

The overlay shows every core Technical box covered, but Build vs Buy and Technical Partnerships are covered only by the PM going on leave. The team marks both as single-owner risks.

Instead of hiring, the lead reallocates: the other two PMs each shadow one live build-versus-buy decision before the leave starts. The next chart shows both boxes covered by two people.

## Ratings that disagree reveal a definition problem

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: during calibration, a PM rates herself strong in Business Case and her manager rates her not yet.

**Walkthrough:**

They compare examples. She points to one-page proposals she writes for each feature. The manager expected a financial model reviewed by finance. The difference comes from the definition rather than the skill.

The team rewrites the Business Case definition to name the expected output, and she re-rates herself as capable. The conversation ends with a development action: write one full business case with finance next quarter.
