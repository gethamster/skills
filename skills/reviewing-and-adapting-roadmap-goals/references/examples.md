# Examples: Reviewing and Adapting GO Roadmap Goals

## A quarterly review that changes the next goal

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team building a personal-finance app ends a quarter whose goal was "help new users connect their first bank account and grow the active base". The next goal on the roadmap is to launch budgeting insights.

**Walkthrough:**

The pre-read shows the connection goal was met for most banks, but users of two regional banks still fail to connect, and support tickets from them are rising. The competition section notes that a rival has launched free budgeting insights, which weakens the case for the next goal as written.

The group keeps the connection goal open for the two banks as a short follow-up and rewrites the next goal around a benefit the rival does not offer, helping couples manage shared spending. The metrics for the new goal are sharpened, the backlog is refocused on it, and the product manager sends a short note to sales and support explaining the change and its reasons.

## A goal missed for a good reason

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team building an online course platform set the goal "help instructors publish their first course within a week and grow the number of active instructors". At the review, the metric shows the goal was missed.

**Walkthrough:**

Instead of labelling the quarter a failure, the group looks at what they learned. Instructors who used the new publishing flow did publish faster. The problem was that far fewer instructors reached the flow than expected, because the sign-up page was confusing.

The group decides the goal remains right and extends it by six weeks, adding one feature aimed at the sign-up page. The date change is announced to the stakeholders who were counting on the original timing. The record of the review explains the reasoning, so the next review can check whether the extra time worked.

## An extra review triggered by a KPI drop

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team maintaining a team-chat product reviews its roadmap quarterly. Midway through a quarter, the weekly KPI check shows message volume falling sharply among large customers.

**Walkthrough:**

The product manager calls an extra review rather than waiting. The investigation shows that a recent performance regression makes the app slow in large workspaces. The current goal, improving search, is paused, and a short goal to restore performance for large workspaces takes its place.

The team updates the roadmap, refocuses the backlog, and tells stakeholders why search is delayed. At the regular quarterly review, performance has recovered and the search goal resumes with its original metrics.
