# Examples: Industry Rivalry Assessment Using Qualitative Research

## Concentrated but fierce

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team assesses rivalry among 4 vendors of payroll software for mid-sized firms in one country. The top two hold most of the market, so the team expects moderate rivalry.

**Walkthrough:**

Structural data shows a concentrated market with slow growth. Interviews with eight recent buyers and three salespeople tell a sharper story: every renewal involves a competing quote, vendors offer free migration and months of discounts to switch, and one vendor matched a rival's price cut within weeks. Coding the interviews shows price matching and switching offers as the dominant themes.

The team rates rivalry high despite concentration, naming slow growth and aggressive switching offers as the drivers. Triggers include any vendor raising prices and a rumored acquisition of the smallest vendor.

## Crowded but calm

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a market for specialized design plug-ins has dozens of small vendors. The team assumes rivalry is intense.

**Walkthrough:**

Interviews show that most vendors serve distinct niches and customers rarely compare them directly. Prices are stable, and buyers choose based on the specific feature they need. Growth is steady. The team rates rivalry low to medium and notes that rivalry is product-based, with little price competition. The main trigger is a platform owner launching its own versions of popular plug-ins, which would also count as a new entrant.

## Using public signals when interviews are limited

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a new strategy analyst has one week and can arrange only two interviews.

**Walkthrough:**

She reviews competitors' pricing pages over the past year, announcements and job postings, and searches competitors' filings for mentions of pricing pressure. Two vendors cut prices and one added a free tier. The two interviews confirm that customers noticed. She rates rivalry medium to high with low confidence and lists the missing interviews as a gap for the next update.
