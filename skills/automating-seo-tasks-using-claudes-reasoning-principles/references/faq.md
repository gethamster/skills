# FAQ: Automate SEO with Claude: Reasoning Principles

## Can Claude look up search volumes or rankings itself?

Not unless you connect it to a tool or give it the data. Without that, any figure it offers is an estimate at best. Export volumes from a keyword tool and rankings or queries from Search Console, then ask Claude to analyze them.

## Is automating SEO with Claude against Google's guidelines?

Google says appropriate use of AI or automation is not against its guidelines, and that using automation primarily to manipulate rankings is. Audits, analysis and improvements to pages people already use fall on the acceptable side. Mass-producing pages to capture keywords falls on the other.

## Which SEO tasks are the best fit for Claude?

Tasks where the input is data you already have and the output is easy to check: title and description audits, grouping queries by intent, spotting overlapping pages, summarizing crawl issues, drafting briefs from research you supply. Tasks that require facts Claude cannot see, such as a competitor's traffic, are a poor fit without a data source.

## How do I stop Claude from inventing facts in an audit?

Tell it to use only the supplied data, to separate what the data shows from what it infers, and to say when a conclusion needs data that is not present. Ask for an evidence column on every row. Then review the output against the source, since instructions reduce errors without removing them.

## Should Claude make changes to the site directly?

Keep a human approval step before anything goes live. Claude can prepare the changes, but a reviewer should confirm them, and changes should go through the same publishing process as any other edit. That keeps mistakes reversible and accountable.
