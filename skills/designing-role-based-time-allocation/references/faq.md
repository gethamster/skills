# FAQ: Role-Based Time Allocation for Business Owners

## What is the right split between the three roles?

There is no universal split. The commonly quoted E-Myth figures describe the typical owner Gerber was diagnosing. They are not a recommended target. Set your own target from your stage, team and goals, and choose a shift you can make this quarter with the people and systems you have.

## Should I block time by role or by task?

By role, with tasks listed inside each block. Role blocks keep the kinds of attention separate, which is the reason for the exercise. The agenda for each block then names the specific tasks and outputs.

## What if my work is too reactive to block?

Start with one protected block and one emergency rule rather than a full template. Clustering reactive work into set windows, the way Paul Graham uses office hours, often reduces interruptions more than people expect. Add blocks as the first one holds.

## How do I handle clients who want me any time?

Offer specific availability windows and hold to them. Explain the change once, and give clients a route for true emergencies. Most clients prefer predictable responsiveness to constant availability with slower delivery.

## How often should I change the template?

Adjust details at each weekly or biweekly review, and reset the target once a quarter. Change the template sooner after a big event, such as a key hire or a lost client. Frequent small corrections work better than occasional overhauls.
