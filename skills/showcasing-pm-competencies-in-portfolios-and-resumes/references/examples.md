# Examples: Showcasing PM Competencies in Portfolios and Resumes

## A delivery PM applying for a senior consumer role

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a PM with several years on a consumer app wants a senior PM role. Her resume is a list of features shipped.

**Walkthrough:**

She maps a few senior consumer job descriptions onto the grid and finds the target region in the upper-left: Opportunity Discovery, Market Positioning, Jobs to be Done and Product Roadmap. Her evidence bank shows plenty of Backlog Grooming and Product Requirements work, plus two projects where she led discovery for a new segment and proposed the roadmap that followed.

She moves those two projects to the top of the resume and rewrites them with the X-Y-Z formula, naming the discovery and roadmap work. The feature list shrinks to two bullets. Her portfolio gets one case study on the segment discovery, focused on the options she compared and why she chose one.

## An engineer moving into a platform PM role

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a senior engineer wants to become a platform PM. He worries that he has no product experience to show.

**Walkthrough:**

Mapping platform PM job descriptions shows the target region on the right of the chart: Build vs Buy, Technical Partnerships, System Analysis and Internal Tools Requirements. His evidence bank already has a vendor evaluation he led, requirements he wrote for an internal tool, and a partnership with a cloud provider's team.

He writes bullets that describe those as product decisions, with results such as reduced support load for internal teams. His summary describes him as a technical PM for internal platforms. His portfolio case study covers the build-versus-buy decision.

## Tailoring one history for two role types

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a PM applying to both growth and core product roles has one resume for both.

**Walkthrough:**

Mapping shows the growth roles cluster in Market boxes such as Conversion Optimization and A/B Testing, while the core roles cluster in Users and Core Product boxes. The PM keeps one evidence bank and makes two versions of the resume.

The growth version leads with an experiment program and its measured effect on activation. The core version leads with a customer research project and the roadmap it shaped. Both share the same summary structure, adjusted to name the region each role needs.
