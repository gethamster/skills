# FAQ: Optimizing Content Presentation for Generative Search

## Does improving fluency reliably increase visibility in AI answers?

Fluency was one of the interventions the original study tested, but the strongest reported gains came from adding citations, quotations and statistics. A later critical review found that generic heuristics transfer poorly across settings. Treat a fluency pass as worth testing on your own pages, not as a guaranteed lift. Measure before and after with the same prompts and engines.

## What is the single most useful presentation edit?

Moving the direct answer earlier on the page is the best-supported choice. Context position was one of the most reproducible levers in the reviewed research, and the primary visibility metric weights content that appears earlier in the answer. It is also cheap and low risk. Do it before investing in tone or vocabulary rewrites.

## Can presentation edits get my page into AI answers if it is not showing up at all?

Usually not. The foundational results were measured on sources already present in the engine's context, so they describe representation, not discovery. If your page is not retrieved for target prompts, work on topical relevance and retrieval first. Return to presentation once the page is in the source set.

## How should I balance an authoritative tone with accuracy?

Remove vague, reflexive hedges but keep specific conditions and real uncertainty. Engines may repeat your sentences closely, so a confident claim must be true without the qualifiers you cut. In regulated or high-stakes topics, add a subject-matter review to every tone edit. An authoritative page that is wrong does more harm than a cautious one.

## Should I add technical terms to every page?

Only where they make a passage more precise for the actual audience. Use the query's own wording and the field's standard terms, define specialist words once, and avoid repetition. Replacing the plain phrases people type with jargon can hurt retrieval. Adding terms alongside plain language is safer than substituting them.
