# FAQ: Now Next Later Roadmap Template

## Which tool is best for a Now Next Later roadmap template?

The one your team already opens every day. Whiteboards suit teams that plan in workshops, spreadsheets suit teams that want filtering and exports, and dedicated roadmapping tools add links between initiatives, ideas and objectives. The structure of the template matters more than the tool. Avoid tools that insist on a date for every item.

## Should the template include dates?

Only as an optional field for real external deadlines, such as a regulatory date or a contractual commitment. A mandatory date field turns the board back into a timeline. Delivery dates for validated work belong in the release plan.

## Can we rename the columns?

Yes. ProdPad's conversion guide lists alternatives such as Current, Near Term and Future, and some teams use labels closer to their old quarters while they transition. Whatever the names, define each column by confidence and put the definition on the board.

## How much text should a card hold?

As little as the column needs. Now cards need enough for the team to act: problem, owner, scope and success measure. Next cards need the problem, the evidence so far and the open questions. Later cards can be a single sentence and an objective tag.

## How do we stop the template from going stale?

Show the last review date and owner on the board, tie updates to a regular review, and keep a single source that all audience views read from. A template people see updated regularly gets trusted and used.
