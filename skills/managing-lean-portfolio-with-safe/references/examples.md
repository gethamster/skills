# Examples: Managing a Lean Portfolio in SAFe

## Replacing an annual budget committee

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a company with four release trains funds initiatives through an annual steering committee, and mid-year ideas have no path to approval.

**Walkthrough:**

The portfolio lead maps existing decision rights and finds the annual committee, a quarterly architecture board and two executives with informal veto power. The LPM group is formed with the committee's funding authority, and the architecture board's approval becomes an exit criterion for the analysis state. A Portfolio Kanban goes live with funnel, review, analysis, implementation and done. Within the first quarter, two mid-year epics move from funnel to a go decision without waiting for the annual cycle.

The old committee meets one last time to hand over its open items.

## Unclogging an overloaded analysis column

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a portfolio has eleven epics marked as in analysis, and none has reached a decision in months.

**Walkthrough:**

The LPM group sets a limit of four epics in analysis, based on how many its two architects can support. It asks each sponsor which of their epics matters most and moves the rest back to review. The four remaining epics each get a four-week time-box and an MVP definition requirement. Three reach go decisions and one is stopped for weak strategic fit.

The next epics are pulled from review only as slots open.

## Stopping an epic mid-implementation

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an approved epic to build a self-service portal has shipped its MVP, but usage after two months is far below the hypothesis.

**Walkthrough:**

At the regular Kanban review, the epic's sponsor presents the usage data against the original expected outcome. The LPM group compares the remaining planned investment with a competing epic waiting at the go decision. It decides to stop further investment in the portal, keep the MVP running, and move the epic to stopped with the reason recorded. The freed capacity goes to the waiting epic, and the trade-off is shown on the board.

The sponsor keeps the option to bring a revised hypothesis back through the funnel.
