# FAQ: Using the North Star Framework for Roadmap Prioritization

## Is the North Star Framework a prioritization framework?

Not by itself. The playbook lists prioritization frameworks among the things the North Star is not, while giving tips for using the North Star when prioritizing. This skill adds a simple scoring model on top of those tips.

## How do we score input influence without perfect data?

Use what you have: historical correlations, cohort comparisons and the reasoning in your input map. Where data is missing, rate by judgment and write down why. Reviews of finished bets will sharpen the ratings over time.

## Where does effort fit in?

It matters, but as a constraint rather than the main score. The playbook suggests paying more attention to forecasted value and how easily work can be tested than to precise duration estimates. Use effort to check capacity and to push large items toward smaller bets.

## Can we keep using RICE or another scoring method?

Yes. Define impact in that method as the expected effect on a named input, and let confidence reflect both links in the chain: the work moving the input and the input moving the North Star. The key is that every score points at an input.

## How often should the ranking change?

Re-rank at least every planning cycle and whenever a review changes what you believe about an input. Keep detail only for the next item and the one in progress so re-ranking stays cheap.
