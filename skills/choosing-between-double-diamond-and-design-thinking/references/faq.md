# FAQ: Double Diamond vs Design Thinking

## Are the Double Diamond and design thinking the same thing?

They share roots and most methods but emphasise different things. The Double Diamond describes the shape of a project, with an explicit problem phase and solution phase. Design thinking, as taught by the d.school, describes modes and a toolkit you can move between freely. Many teams use the Double Diamond as the plan and design thinking methods inside it.

## Where does Lean UX fit?

Lean UX suits teams already building a product in short iterations. It frames work as assumptions and experiments. It works well inside the second diamond once the problem is agreed, and less well for open-ended discovery.

## Can we combine all three?

Yes, and it is common. Use one as the backbone for phases and decisions, and borrow methods from the others. Avoid running two backbones at once, which confuses stakeholders about where the project stands.

## Which framework is best for a beginner team?

The Double Diamond is usually the easiest to explain and follow, because it has four named phases and a clear output at each narrowing point. Add design thinking methods gradually as the team gains confidence.

## How do we know we chose wrong?

Warning signs include stakeholders repeatedly asking where the project is, the team skipping the framework's steps under pressure, or decision points that never line up with approvals. Review the choice at a planned checkpoint and adjust.
