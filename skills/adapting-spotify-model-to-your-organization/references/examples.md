# Examples: Adapting the Spotify Model to Your Organization

## Scoping the adoption to real problems

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: A software company with eight delivery teams wants to "go Spotify" after leadership reads about it. Teams report two main frustrations: waiting on a central QA group and inconsistent frontend standards.

**Walkthrough:**

The change lead lists the problems and finds no evidence of siloed knowledge or too many teams to coordinate. She proposes cross-functional squads that include testers, which targets the QA wait, and a frontend chapter, which targets inconsistent standards. Tribes and guilds are left out because nothing on the problem list needs them. The one-page design explains each choice.

Teams react more positively than expected, because the proposal describes their own complaints rather than a foreign vocabulary.

## Recovering buy-in after a top-down launch

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: A retailer announced squads and tribes company-wide in one memo. Three months later, engineers describe the change as renaming, and retrospective time has roughly doubled across teams.

**Walkthrough:**

The new sponsor pauses further rollout and runs listening sessions with each squad. She rewrites the rationale around two problems teams actually named: slow approvals for releases and unclear ownership of the checkout service. Two volunteer squads receive real release authority for a quarter, and ceremony time is capped. A scorecard agreed with engineering, product and finance tracks lead time, incidents, debt and team health.

At the review, the pilot squads show fewer incidents and better health scores, and other teams ask to join.

## Measuring beyond flow

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: A fintech's pilot squads cut cycle time noticeably, and executives want to scale immediately. Squad members privately report skipped testing and growing debt.

**Walkthrough:**

The change lead points to the agreed scorecard, which also includes technical debt and defect escape rate. Both have worsened during the pilot. Rather than scaling, the team adds a shared definition of done and reserves a slice of capacity for debt. The next review shows cycle time holding at the improved level while defects return to baseline.

Scaling then proceeds with support from both leadership and squads.
