# Examples: Identifying Cross-Journey Insights and Patterns

## One status message behind many complaints

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a home services company has researched maps for booking a job, rescheduling, paying, and making a complaint. Each journey owner has a list of local fixes.

**Walkthrough:**

After tagging, the pattern matrix shows one tag, "unclear job status," in all four journeys. Tracing the evidence reveals a single cause: technicians update job status in a system that customers never see, so customers call to ask.

The insight goes into the register with the field operations lead as owner and all four journeys listed as affected. In prioritization it outranks every single-journey fix, because one change to status notifications addresses pain in four places.

## A promise broken two journeys later

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a software company's acquisition journey promises that customers can import their data in minutes. Onboarding research shows many customers struggling with imports.

**Walkthrough:**

The expectation-gap check compares the acquisition journey's promises with what onboarding delivers. The import promise appears on the pricing page and in sales demos, while the import tool only handles simple cases without help.

The insight is recorded with two possible responses: change the promise or improve the tool. Marketing and product owners review it together, and the prioritization cycle weighs a short-term wording change against a longer investment in the importer.

## A redundancy nobody had noticed

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an insurer compares its quote, purchase, and first-claim journeys for the first time. Each journey owner considers their data collection necessary.

**Walkthrough:**

The redundancy search shows customers are asked for the same vehicle details in all three journeys, because each team built its own form on a different system. No single map flagged it, since each asks only once.

The insight is assigned to the owner of the customer data platform. The fix is to carry details forward between journeys, and the register records a follow-up check of completion times in each journey after the change ships.
