# FAQ: How to Emerge from Technician to Entrepreneur

## Do I have to stop doing technical work completely?

No. Many founders keep a small part of the craft, often the part that most depends on their judgment or the part they value most. The aim is that the business no longer depends on you doing the work, so you choose what you keep instead of keeping everything by default.

## How long does the transition take?

It depends on your team, your systems and how much of the work is judgment-heavy. Expect several months for the first meaningful shift and longer for the identity change. Quarterly reassessments show whether you are moving.

## What if my delegate does the work differently from me?

Judge the result against the written standard rather than your own habits. If the result meets the standard, accept it. If it does not, improve the process or the training before stepping back in.

## How do I delegate work that needs my judgment?

Split the decision from the execution. Hand off the execution first, keep the decision, and write down how you make it. Over time the written criteria let a trusted person make more of those decisions, starting with the common cases.

## What if clients only want me?

Introduce the person taking over while you are still involved and explain what stays with you. Keep your presence at key moments, such as the first consultation or final review, and let the delegate own the day-to-day work. Most clients stay if the quality holds.
