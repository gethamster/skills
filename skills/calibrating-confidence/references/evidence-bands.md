# Confidence Evidence Bands

Map confidence percentages to types of evidence, not feelings of certainty.

| % | Band | Typical evidence |
|---|------|------------------|
| 100% | Validated | Production data, completed experiment, signed customer commitment |
| 80% | Strong | Adjacent product data, qualitative research with multiple users, A/B precedent in a similar surface |
| 50% | Plausible | Stakeholder anecdote, a single user interview, internal team belief |
| 20% | Speculative | Guess, "feels right", market story without measurement |

## When to Update

Recalibrate Confidence whenever new evidence arrives—not only at quarter boundaries. Ship results, experiment readouts, and clearer analytics all warrant re-scoring mid-cycle.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Default 80%.** If most of your scores cluster at 80%, the rubric is decorative. Force at least some scores down to 50% or 20%.
- **Score by enthusiasm.** Excitement is not evidence. Score the data, not the room.
- **Single-source confidence.** One customer asking for a feature is 50%, not 80%.
