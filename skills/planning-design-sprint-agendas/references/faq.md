# FAQ: Planning a Design Sprint Agenda and Schedule

## How far ahead should we plan a design sprint?

Google's Design Sprint Kit advises at least one full day of planning for each day of sprinting ([Design Sprint Kit](https://designsprintkit.withgoogle.com/planning/overview)). Much of that time goes to securing the Decider, booking experts and recruiting customers. Start recruiting as early as you can, since customer availability is the hardest thing to control.

## What does a design sprint agenda look like?

The published five-day agenda runs Monday to Friday: map and target, sketch, decide and storyboard, prototype, and test. The checklist from Jake Knapp and John Zeratsky gives approximate times for every activity, with breaks every sixty to ninety minutes and a late lunch ([Design Sprint guide](https://www.character.vc/guide/design-sprint)). A four-day agenda moves mapping and sketching into one day and the decision and storyboard into the next.

## Should we run a four-day or a five-day sprint?

For a first sprint, Knapp and Zeratsky recommend the full five days, saying teams need that time to plan, build a solid prototype and find real customers. AJ&Smart's four-day Design Sprint 2.0 is aimed at teams, including large organisations, that cannot give a full week ([AJ&Smart](https://www.facilitator.com/blog/design-sprint-101)). Whichever you choose, keep every phase, including the customer test.

## What supplies do we need?

For an in-person sprint: whiteboards, yellow sticky notes, black pens and markers, printer paper, small and large dot stickers in two different colors, masking tape and timers. The checklist lists quantities and brands if you want to follow it exactly. For a remote sprint you need an online whiteboard, a video tool and a template set up in advance.

## Who needs to attend the whole sprint?

The core team, including the Decider and Facilitator, should attend the whole sprint. Google's kit says participants need to be present for the entire sprint, while experts may join only for specific exercises ([Design Sprint Kit FAQ](https://designsprintkit.withgoogle.com/methodology/faq)). If the Decider cannot attend fully, they should name a delegate who can decide on their behalf.
