# FAQ: Launching and Running Agile Release Trains

## How do I know the value stream is the right basis for a train?

Check whether a typical feature could move from idea to customer release using only the people and systems inside the proposed boundary. If most features still need outside groups, the boundary follows the org chart rather than the value stream. Adjust by pulling missing capabilities inside or by redrawing the boundary around a different product area.

## Should teams be reorganized before the first PI Planning event?

Yes, form the teams before planning so each team plans its own work with the people who will do it. Planning with provisional teams produces commitments nobody owns. Keep the new teams stable for at least a couple of increments before judging whether the design works.

## What happens if the train depends heavily on a shared service outside it?

Record the shared service as an explicit external dependency with a named contact, and invite its representatives to PI Planning. If the dependency blocks most features, consider embedding people from that service in the train or making it part of the boundary. Persistent heavy dependencies are a sign the boundary needs rework.

## When should one train become two, or join a solution train?

Split when a single planning event can no longer coordinate everyone effectively, or when dependencies cluster into separate groups with little crossover. If several trains must deliver one large solution together, coordinate them as a [solution train](https://tryhamster.com/skills/coordinating-multiple-agile-release-trains). Base the decision on the dependency board, not on headcount alone.

## Do we need the full framework in place before launching?

No. A sound value stream boundary, stable cross-functional teams, clear roles and a ready backlog matter more than adopting every practice at once. Scaled Agile's [CSG International case study](https://scaledagile.com/case_study/csg-international) describes a transformation that started before SAFe was codified, and practices can be added as the train learns.
