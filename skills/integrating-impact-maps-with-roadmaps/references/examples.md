# Examples: Integrating Impact Maps with Product Roadmaps

## Example: SaaS Onboarding Improvement Roadmap

**Scenario:**

A B2B SaaS product manager has completed an impact map with the goal 'Increase 30-day activation rate from 28% to 50%.' The map identifies three key actors: trial admins, end users invited by admins, and customer success managers. Multiple impacts and deliverables have been generated and prioritized.

**Walkthrough:**

The PM clusters deliverables into three impact themes: (1) 'Accelerate admin time-to-first-value' targeting trial admins — includes guided setup wizard, sample data import, and first-dashboard template. (2) 'Increase end-user adoption within trial accounts' targeting invited users — includes simplified invite flow and role-based onboarding paths. (3) 'Enable CS-driven activation for high-value accounts' targeting CSMs — includes trial health dashboard and automated intervention triggers.

Theme 1 goes to 'Now' because admin activation has the strongest validated data (an experiment showed 60% of admins who complete setup in day 1 convert). Theme 2 goes to 'Next' with an experiment planned to test whether simplified invites actually increase end-user engagement. Theme 3 goes to 'Later' because CSM capacity constraints mean the infrastructure isn't ready yet.

The roadmap presentation to leadership leads with: 'Our goal is 50% 30-day activation. Our highest-leverage move is getting admins to value in their first session. Here's our plan and how we'll measure each phase.' Each theme card shows the target metric, the actor, and the 2-3 deliverables within it.

## Example: Marketplace Seller Engagement Roadmap

**Scenario:**

A product manager at an online marketplace completed an impact map with the goal 'Grow active seller listings by 40% in 12 months.' Actors include new sellers, dormant sellers (inactive 60+ days), and the internal seller operations team. The map has 14 deliverables across 6 impacts.

**Walkthrough:**

After prioritization scoring, 8 deliverables survive. The PM clusters them into two themes: (1) 'Reduce listing friction for new sellers' (impacts: simplify first listing flow, provide pricing guidance) with 4 deliverables. (2) 'Reactivate dormant sellers through performance visibility' (impacts: show dormant sellers what they're missing, surface easy re-listing opportunities) with 4 deliverables.

Six remaining deliverables targeting the ops team are placed in 'Later' as one theme — 'Scale seller support through automation' — because they require a platform investment not yet approved.

The product manager roadmap uses outcome metrics: Theme 1 measures 'new sellers with 3+ listings in first 14 days,' and Theme 2 measures 'dormant sellers re-listing within 30 days of intervention.' The Now/Next/Later format lets the PM present Theme 1 as the current focus while positioning Theme 2 as ready-to-start once a pending A/B test on the re-engagement email validates the core assumption.
