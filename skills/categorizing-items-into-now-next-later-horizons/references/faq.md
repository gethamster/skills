# FAQ: Now Next Later Categorization

## How do I decide between Now and Next for an item?

Ask whether the team has a solution it trusts and understands the effort. If the problem is validated but the solution is still open, the item belongs in Next, where discovery can narrow the options. If the solution has been tested enough to commit to and engineering has a real sense of the work, it can go in Now. When in doubt, choose Next and write down what would move it.

## Should important items always go in Now?

No. Importance decides the order within a column, and confidence decides the column. A critical strategic bet with little evidence belongs at the top of Later or Next, where its importance tells the team to start discovery on it. Putting it in Now before it is understood usually leads to rework.

## How many items should each horizon hold?

There is no fixed number in Bastow's writing. The guidance is qualitative: Now should hold what the team can realistically focus on, and a long Now column signals weak prioritization. The whole board should be readable in one or two screens. Size each column to your team's capacity and to how much discovery it can run.

## What if stakeholders disagree with where I placed their item?

Ask what evidence supports a different placement. If they have evidence you did not, move the item and thank them. If they do not, show the rationale line and the evidence the item would need to move. Graduation criteria written in advance make these conversations much shorter.

## How often should we recategorize the whole backlog?

A full recategorization is usually needed only when you first adopt the format or when strategy changes significantly. After that, items move one at a time in regular reviews as evidence changes. The reprioritization skill covers that ongoing process.
