# Examples: Convert Stage

## A homewares store shows delivery costs earlier

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a small homewares store sees many shoppers reach the checkout and leave at the delivery step. The team suspects the price of the products.

**Walkthrough:**

A short exit survey on the checkout asks what stopped the purchase. Most answers mention delivery charges they had not expected. This matches one of the most common causes in Baymard's research.

The team shows delivery costs on every product page and in the basket, and adds a free delivery threshold with a note in the basket showing how close the shopper is. They test the basket message against the old version, one change at a time. The share of checkouts that reach payment rises, and average order value rises with it.

## An insurance broker cuts its quote form

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an online insurance broker's quote form asks for a long list of details before showing any price. Many visitors start the form and never finish it.

**Walkthrough:**

The funnel shows the steepest drop on the second page of the form. The team reviews every field with the underwriting team and removes the fields that are not needed for an indicative quote, moving them to after the price is shown.

They run the shorter form as an A/B test with a fixed run time decided in advance. Completed quotes rise. The team then checks the next stage, policies bought from quotes, to make sure the extra quotes are not all low-intent.

## A B2B software vendor speeds up proposals

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a B2B software vendor's Convert stage runs through sales. Many prospects who request pricing never sign.

**Walkthrough:**

The CRM funnel shows prospects waiting several days between a pricing request and a proposal. Lost-deal notes mention competitors who replied faster. The team treats this as a Convert problem and sets a target response time for proposals.

Marketing builds a standard proposal template with pricing tiers and case studies, so sales can send one the same day. A follow-up rule reminds reps to contact prospects who have opened a proposal but not replied. Convert KPIs now include time to proposal alongside win rate.
