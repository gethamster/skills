# FAQ: Convert Stage

## What is the Convert stage in the RACE Framework?

It is the decision stage, where a prospect becomes a paying customer. Dave Chaffey defines it as conversion to sale in online or offline channels, whether payment happens through ecommerce or offline. It follows Act, where the prospect first interacted, and leads into Engage.

## Where should conversion rate optimization start?

Start with measurement. Map the steps of your decision funnel, find the step with the largest drop weighted by value, and collect evidence on why people leave there. Only then decide on fixes. Changes made without a baseline cannot be judged.

## How do I approach cart abandonment recovery?

First remove the causes you can fix, such as unexpected costs, forced account creation and long checkouts, which are among the common reasons in Baymard Institute's research. Then set up a short reminder sequence for abandoned baskets and re-engagement ads with a frequency cap. Measure recovered orders separately so you know what recovery adds.

## Is retargeting at the decision stage worth it?

Often, because it reaches people who have already shown intent. Google Ads calls these audiences "your data" segments, built from people who previously interacted with your brand. Keep the message relevant to what they looked at, cap the frequency, and compare the cost per recovered conversion with the cost of new customers from other channels.

## How does Convert work when sales happen offline?

The same loop applies to a different funnel: inquiry, response, proposal, negotiation, signature. Measure each step in the CRM, find the biggest drop, and fix its causes, such as slow responses or unclear proposals. Chaffey built offline conversion into RACE for this reason.
