# Examples: Continuous Learning Loops from Field Deployments

## A workaround that turned into a pattern

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an FDE at a freight company notices that planners export the product's route view to a spreadsheet every morning before making changes. Nobody has asked for anything; they just do it.

**Walkthrough:**

The FDE logs it as a workaround, marked "seen," with a screenshot of the spreadsheet and a note that planners need to edit several routes at once. A search of the shared log finds a similar entry from another FDE at a food distributor. The FDE links the two and tags the pair as a possible pattern. At the next product review, the counterpart asks for one more data point, and a third FDE confirms the same behavior a week later. The item moves to "planned," and all three FDEs hear the decision.

## Separating a one-off request from a recurring need

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a hospital customer asks an FDE for an export in an unusual file format required by one of its regional regulators.

**Walkthrough:**

The FDE logs the request as "reported," type missing capability, scope "this customer only," because no other entries mention that regulator. The FDE builds the export inside the engagement as a small custom job and notes it in the decision log. The entry stays open with a note to revisit if another customer in the same region asks. The product review skips it, which keeps the product team's time on patterns.

## Closing the loop with the customer

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a product team ships a bulk editing feature that started as a field log entry from three FDEs at different customers.

**Walkthrough:**

The product counterpart updates the log entries to "shipped" and posts in the FDE channel. Each FDE tells their customer that the feature came from their feedback and shows them how to use it, and then retires the spreadsheet workaround where it existed. The FDEs note in the log how quickly users adopted the feature, which gives the product team evidence that field entries lead to real use.
