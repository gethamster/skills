# FAQ: Start Stop Continue in 1-on-1s and Performance Reviews

## How often should I use start stop continue in 1-on-1s?

Use it as often as it produces useful changes. Some managers use it in every 1-on-1 with a short version, and others use it once a month or at the end of a project. Whatever the cadence, start each use by reviewing the changes agreed last time.

## Who should speak first?

Let the report go first. Hearing their items first shows you how they see their own work and reduces the chance they simply agree with your framing. The manager then adds items the report did not raise.

## Should the report give the manager feedback too?

Yes, if you want the format to feel fair. Invite items about yourself in all three columns and listen without defending. Acknowledge anything you will change and review it at the next meeting.

## Can I use it in a formal performance review?

Yes. Use the same three headings over the whole review period and bring examples from across that period. Keeping notes from each 1-on-1 makes this much easier. Give Continue items as much space as Stop items so the review records strengths clearly.

## What if the report brings nothing for the Stop column?

That can mean they do not see a problem or do not feel safe naming one. Ask a narrower question, such as what takes more of their time than it should. If you have a Stop item for them, share it with a specific situation and ask for their view.
