# Examples: ICE Scoring: Prioritizing Product Ideas by Confidence

## An exciting idea meets the Confidence Meter

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team with an activation goal scores three ideas: an AI assistant for new users, a shorter signup form, and a guided setup checklist. The AI assistant is the favorite in the room.

**Walkthrough:**

Each person scores on their own first. The AI assistant gets high Impact estimates, but when the team lists its evidence, the only support is enthusiasm and industry buzz. On the Confidence Meter that is opinion, so Confidence lands near zero. The shorter signup form has analytics showing a large drop-off on one field, which is data, so its Confidence is higher. The checklist has a handful of customer requests behind it.

Illustrative scenario: the team's scores on a 0-10 scale, multiplied.

| Idea | Impact | Confidence | Ease | ICE |
|------|--------|------------|------|-----|
| AI assistant | 9 | 0.1 | 2 | 1.8 |
| Shorter signup form | 4 | 2 | 8 | 64 |
| Setup checklist | 6 | 0.5 | 6 | 18 |

The signup form goes into a step-project first. The AI assistant is not discarded: the team plans a cheap test of whether new users would use it, which could raise its Confidence at the next re-score.

## Scores diverge and reveal missing information

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: during independent scoring of a "bulk edit" idea, engineers give Ease a low score and the designer gives it a high one.

**Walkthrough:**

The facilitator asks both sides to explain. The designer was picturing a simple multi-select, while the engineers were picturing edits across every field, with undo and permissions. The gap was two different ideas under one name.

The team splits the entry into two ideas in the bank and scores each separately. The simple version ranks higher and is chosen for a first step-project, and the fuller version is parked with a note pointing to the first test.

## Re-scoring after a test

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a team ran a fake-door test for a new export feature that had ranked second. A button was shown to a sample of users, and clicks were recorded.

**Walkthrough:**

Very few users clicked, well below the threshold the team had set before the test. Confidence in the Impact estimate drops, since test results are the strongest evidence type, and the idea falls down the ranking. The team records the result and the reason in the idea bank.

The idea that had ranked third moves up and gets the next step-project. The export idea is parked with the test result attached, so the next person to propose it will see what was already learned.
