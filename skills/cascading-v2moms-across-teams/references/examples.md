# Examples: Cascading V2MOMs from Leadership to Individual Contributors

## One company method cascades three levels

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a software company's V2MOM lists method M2, "become the default choice for mid-sized healthcare customers," with measure MS2, "sign 30 new healthcare accounts this year."

**Walkthrough:**

The sales department takes M2 as the basis for its vision and writes methods such as "hire two healthcare account executives" (points to M2) and "build a healthcare reference program" (points to M2). The product department picks up M2 with "pass the security review healthcare buyers require" and names the compliance team's capacity as an obstacle. At the team level, the security engineering team writes a V2MOM whose top method is the security review work, with a measure tied to the product department's measure. An individual engineer on that team writes a method to own the audit logging work. Tracing the engineer's measure upward reaches MS2 in three steps.

## A side-by-side review finds an orphan and a gap

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: five department V2MOMs are read together at a cross-department review.

**Walkthrough:**

The reviewers list every department method with its parent code. One marketing method, "launch a podcast," has no parent code, and the marketing lead admits it was carried over from last year. It is dropped. The reviewers then check each company method for owners and find that M4, "reduce time to first value for new customers," has no department method pointing to it. The company V2MOM owner assigns it to customer success, which adds a method and a measure, and the change is recorded so teams below can pick it up.

## Feedback from below changes the company V2MOM

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: while cascading, several engineering teams list the same obstacle: the platform migration planned in the company V2MOM uses the same engineers as the top product launch.

**Walkthrough:**

Read one at a time, each team's obstacle looks local. Read together, they show a conflict in the company V2MOM itself. The engineering lead brings the pattern to the leadership team, which reranks the methods so the launch comes first and moves the migration start to the second half. The company V2MOM is updated, the method codes stay the same, and the affected teams update their own documents at the next review.
