# FAQ: Running Chapters for Discipline-Based Management

## Who does a chapter member report to?

A chapter member reports to the chapter lead for line management. [At Spotify, every squad member is assigned to a chapter, with a chapter lead who serves, in effect, as a line manager](https://cisr.mit.edu/publication/2017_1201_DigitalDesignAtSpotify_BaiyereRossSebastian). Day-to-day priorities still come from the squad. Making both relationships explicit avoids members feeling they have two bosses for the same decisions.

## Does the chapter lead still do hands-on work?

Yes. The [Spotify paper](https://storage.ghost.io/c/73/a9/73a90ce4-1663-4169-a7cb-efdf906b6b25/content/files/2022/12/Scaling-Agile-@-Spotify-with-Tribes--Squads--Chapters---Guilds-Henrik-Kniberg---Anders-Ivarsson-Oct-2012.pdf) describes the lead as part of a squad and involved in its day-to-day work. This keeps them close to how their reports actually work. Their delivery load should be reduced to leave room for coaching and management.

## How big should a chapter be?

Sources disagree. One guide suggests [5-15 people](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/project-management/spotify-model) and another says chapters typically hold [5-20 people](https://mooncamp.com/glossary/spotify-model). The practical limit is how many people the lead can coach while doing squad work. If one-to-ones start slipping, the chapter is too large.

## What should a chapter be responsible for?

Typical remits cover [coding standards, interview loops, onboarding and technical direction](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/project-management/spotify-model), plus the lead's people-management duties of coaching, performance, hiring and standards. Delivery priorities stay with squads. Write the remit down so both sides know where decisions sit.

## What happens in chapter meetings?

Chapter meetings let specialists discuss their own technical topics and [coordinate technologies, tools, processes and methods](https://usu.com/en/blog/the-spotify-model-magic-bullet-or-overrated). They are also where members [share solutions to common problems](https://productschool.com/blog/product-fundamentals/spotify-model-scaling-agile) found in different squads. Keep squad status updates out of them.
