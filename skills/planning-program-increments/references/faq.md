# FAQ: Planning Program Increments \(PI Planning\)

## How long does PI planning take?

Many trains run it over two consecutive days, which gives room for two breakouts and a management review in between. Smaller or more mature trains sometimes compress it, but cutting breakout time is the usual cause of shallow plans. Preparation before the event, such as feature refinement and capacity calculation, often takes several weeks. Treat the event length as a choice you revisit in the event retrospective.

## What is the difference between PI objectives and features?

Features are items of solution functionality from the backlog that teams plan into stories. PI objectives are short business statements summarizing what each team intends to achieve during the increment. An objective can span several features or capture work, such as an enabler, that is not a feature at all. Business owners weigh objectives, not features, which is why objectives must be written in outcome terms.

## Who should attend PI planning?

Everyone on the train should attend, including every team member, not only leads. Product management, business owners, system architects and the Release Train Engineer each have distinct roles in briefing, answering questions, weighing objectives and facilitating. Leaving developers out produces plans that do not reflect real capacity or technical constraints. Remote members need the same access to briefings, breakouts and the dependency board.

## Can PI planning be run remotely?

Yes, as long as the event keeps its structure of briefings, breakouts and reviews. The shared dependency board and iteration grids must be digital and visible to everyone at once. Build in more time for cross-team conversations, since walking over to another team becomes scheduling a call. Time zones may require splitting days into shorter sessions.

## What happens after PI planning?

Teams execute their iteration plans, and the train tracks progress against the committed objectives and dependencies. The plan is a forecast, so teams adjust within iterations as they learn. At the end of the increment, the [Inspect and Adapt event](https://scaledagileframework.com/glossary) demonstrates and evaluates the solution against what was planned. Lessons from that review shape preparation for the next PI planning.
