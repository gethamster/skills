# FAQ: Now Next Later Framework for SaaS Product Roadmaps

## Does Now Next Later work with continuous deployment?

Yes. The roadmap sits above delivery and tracks problems and direction. Validated ideas move into the delivery backlog and ship through the pipeline at whatever pace it runs. Small improvements can ship without appearing on the roadmap at all, as long as they are traceable to an initiative.

## How should we handle feature requests from large customers?

Log them as evidence against the problem they point to, and weigh them against your objectives and your target segment. A large customer's request can carry real weight, but account size alone should not move an item. When a renewal truly depends on a capability, treat it as a real commitment and date that one initiative.

## Should a SaaS company publish its roadmap?

Many do, and a horizon format makes it safer because it shows problems rather than dated features. ProdPad's public roadmap guidance recommends leaving out dates and exact features, including recently launched work and keeping the public view updated from the internal one. Leave sensitive items off.

## What can sales say about items in Next and Later?

They can describe the problems the team is exploring and the direction of the product. They should not promise features or dates for items in Next or Later. Give sales a short script and a route to request a real commitment when a deal depends on one.

## How do SaaS metrics fit into the roadmap?

Through objectives. Tag each initiative with the objective it serves, such as activation, retention or expansion, and track the related metrics in your OKRs. The roadmap shows the plan to move those metrics; the metrics show whether it is working.
