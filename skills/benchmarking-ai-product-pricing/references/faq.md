# FAQ: Benchmarking AI Product Pricing Against Competitors

## How do I benchmark when competitors hide pricing behind sales?

Use what buyers tell you. Ask in sales calls and win-loss interviews what alternatives quoted, and record it with the date and deal size. Customer review sites and procurement communities also carry reported prices. Mark these rows as lower confidence and look for several data points before relying on them.

## How often should I refresh the benchmark?

On a schedule, such as quarterly, and after any major competitor pricing change. AI prices move faster than traditional software prices because model costs keep falling. A dated archive of pricing pages makes refreshes quick.

## Should I compare against general-purpose AI tools?

Yes, when buyers do. Many buyers compare a specialist product with a general assistant or a direct model API. Include them in the set, normalize their price to your value unit, and map what they lack.

## Why does the benchmark show we are the most expensive, yet we still win deals?

Buyers are paying for something the price comparison does not capture, such as accuracy, integrations or governance. Find out what it is from win interviews and put it on the pricing page. The benchmark is telling you where your value story is working.

## Should I benchmark before or after calculating my own unit economics?

After. You need your own cost per unit to know whether a market price is viable. Benchmarking first tends to anchor the team on competitor prices before anyone knows the cost to serve.
