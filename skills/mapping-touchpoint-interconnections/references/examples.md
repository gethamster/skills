# Examples: Mapping Touchpoint Interconnections Across Journeys

## A notification redesign that would have broken returns

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an online retailer's delivery team plans to redesign its order-status email to reduce "where is my order" contacts. The change looks local to the delivery journey.

**Walkthrough:**

The touchpoint matrix shows the same email template also appears in the returns journey, where it confirms that a refund has been issued, and in the exchange journey. The change-impact rule sends the proposal to both journey owners.

The returns owner points out that the new layout drops the refund amount. The delivery team adds a variant for refunds before launch, and the matrix entry is updated with the date and the variant.

## Finding the repeated question at a handoff

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a software company's customers complain that they have to explain their setup twice, once to sales and again to the onboarding team.

**Walkthrough:**

The team documents the handoff between the "Buy the product" and "Get started" journeys. The list of information that crosses the handoff shows that sales notes stay in the CRM, while onboarding works from a separate project tool with no link to them.

Both owners agree on a short handoff record that moves with the account. The handoff gets a named accountable owner, and the next round of onboarding interviews checks whether customers still repeat themselves.

## A billing system behind many touchpoints

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a telecom's CX team links its most shared touchpoints to backstage systems for the first time. Several touchpoints that seemed unrelated sit in different journeys.

**Walkthrough:**

The dependency view shows that the online bill page, the payment reminder message, and the plan change confirmation all draw on one billing system. A planned migration of that system had been scoped as an internal IT project.

With the dependency recorded, the migration plan now includes a check of every journey those touchpoints appear in. The journey owners are added to the migration's review list before any cutover date is set.
