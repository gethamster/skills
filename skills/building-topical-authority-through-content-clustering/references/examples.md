# Examples: Building Topical Authority With Content Clustering

## A software company that covers too many topics

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an accounting software company publishes two posts a week across tax, payroll, invoicing, hiring and general small business advice. Traffic is spread thin, and no topic has more than a handful of pages.

**Walkthrough:**

The six pillars review picks invoicing as the topic to own, because it matches the product and the team's expertise. The team writes a boundary: invoicing for small businesses, including payment terms, reminders and disputes, excluding tax filing. The completeness checklist lists about twenty pages, of which six already exist in some form.

They publish a pillar page on small business invoicing with five cluster pages, merge two older posts that answered the same question about payment terms, and pause new posts on unrelated topics. Each month they group invoicing queries by page and add missing questions to the checklist. The team sets its goal for the next two quarters as completing the checklist.

## A cluster where two pages compete

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a hiring platform has a pillar page on structured interviews. Search Console shows two cluster pages, one on interview scorecards and one on interview rubrics, trading places for the same queries.

**Walkthrough:**

The team compares the two pages and the search results for their shared queries. The results are nearly identical, which suggests searchers treat scorecards and rubrics as one need. They merge the pages into one guide, redirect the weaker URL, and update the pillar page's link and anchor text.

The monthly review a few months later shows one page holding those queries. The freed writing time goes to a gap on the checklist: a page on training interviewers, which had impressions and no good page.

## Extending to an adjacent topic

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a gardening retailer has completed its checklist for container gardening. Visibility across the topic has been stable for two quarterly reviews.

**Walkthrough:**

The quarterly review confirms there are no open gaps and no competing pages. The six pillars plan lists small space vegetable growing as the next topic. The team writes its boundary, builds its checklist, and identifies which container gardening pages will link to the new pillar.

They publish the new pillar with its first cluster pages and add contextual links from related container pages. The container cluster moves to a lighter schedule of refreshes when content actually changes.
