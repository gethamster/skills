# FAQ: Process in the Marketing Mix

## What does Process mean in the 7 Ps?

Process is the set of activities that delivers the service to the customer, from first contact to follow-up. Because customers take part in many service processes and see them from the inside, the design of the process shapes what they experience and how they judge quality.

## What is a service blueprint?

A service blueprint is a map of a service process with time running across the page. It shows customer actions, what staff do in view of the customer, what happens backstage and the supporting systems, separated by a line of visibility. Fail points, bottlenecks and time standards are marked on it.

## How is service process design different from process improvement in operations?

The tools overlap, but the starting point differs. Service process design in the marketing mix starts from what the customer sees and feels, and judges each step by its effect on the customer and the positioning. Operational improvement often starts from cost and throughput. The best results come from considering both.

## Should we standardize or customize the process?

It depends on the positioning. Standardizing steps and reducing staff discretion suits volume services that promise consistency and speed. Keeping discretion suits premium or tailored services where customers pay for judgement. Most services standardize the steps around the parts where judgement matters.

## How often should we update the blueprint?

Update it whenever the service changes, when complaints or waits rise, and before copying the service to a new location or partner. A blueprint that no longer matches reality misleads the people who use it for training and planning.
