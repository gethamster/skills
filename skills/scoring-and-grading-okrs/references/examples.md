**Scenario:**

A VP of Engineering is reviewing scored OKRs from three teams (Platform, Mobile, and Data) at the end of Q2. Each team has already completed their internal scoring session and submitted scorecards with interpretations. The VP needs to identify cross-team patterns and prepare a summary for the executive team. The organization has 60 engineers and has been using OKRs for six quarters.

This cross-team dependency was not surfaced during the quarter's [check-ins](https://tryhamster.com/skills/conducting-okr-check-ins-and-reviews).

The VP documented this as a systemic issue and proposed that next quarter's planning include explicit dependency mapping between teams, feeding directly into the [alignment process](https://tryhamster.com/skills/aligning-okrs-across-teams). 9 committed miss on Platform told a connected story that an average would have obscured.
