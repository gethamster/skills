# Examples: Measuring FDE Success by Business Outcomes

## Turning a pipeline project into an outcome scorecard

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an FDE is building a data pipeline for a regional distributor. The brief's outcome is that planners stop placing emergency orders caused by stale stock data.

**Walkthrough:**

The FDE defines the measure as emergency orders per month, which the distributor's finance team already tracks. The baseline comes from the last six months of finance records. Leading indicators are the age of stock data when planners open the planning screen, and the share of planners using the new screen. The attribution method is agreed with the sponsor: compare the months after launch with the same months last year and note any supplier disruptions. Pipeline throughput and rows processed are tracked only as diagnostics.

Illustrative scenario: the scorecard at the closing review.

| Measure | Baseline | Result |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Emergency orders per month | 40 | 25 |
| Planners using the new screen | 0 of 20 | 16 of 20 |
| Typical stock data age at planning | 1 day | 1 hour |

## Using evaluations as a leading indicator

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an FDE team is deploying a language model to draft replies for an insurer's support agents. The outcome is shorter handling time per case, which will take weeks to show.

**Walkthrough:**

The team agrees validation criteria with the support lead and builds an evaluation set from labeled past cases, scored by experienced agents. The evaluation score becomes the main leading indicator during the first weeks, reported weekly next to draft acceptance by agents. Handling time is reported on a slower rhythm once enough cases have gone through. When evaluation scores stall on one claim type, the team narrows rollout to the types that pass and records the gap for the product team.

## Reporting a missed target honestly

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: an engagement aimed to reduce invoice processing time for a manufacturer's finance team. At the closing review, the leading indicators looked healthy, but the outcome moved less than the target.

**Walkthrough:**

The FDE presents the result in the same scorecard format as always. Adoption was high, but a quarter-end reorganization moved half the team to other work during the measurement window, which the attribution notes had listed as a risk. The FDE shows the result for the part of the team that stayed, labels it as partial evidence, and proposes a short follow-on measurement period instead of claiming success. The sponsor agrees, and the finding goes into the field report.
