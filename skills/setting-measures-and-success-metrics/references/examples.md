# Examples: Setting V2MOM Measures and Success Metrics

## A vague measure becomes a checkable one

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a customer success team's V2MOM method is "help new customers reach their first successful project faster." The first draft measure reads "improve onboarding."

**Walkthrough:**

The team asks what would be observably different if the method worked. The answer is that new customers would finish their first project sooner. They pull the current figure from their product data: today, 40 of every 100 new accounts finish a first project within their first month. The measure becomes "raise first-month project completion from 40 to 55 of every 100 new accounts by the end of the third quarter," owned by the team lead, sourced from the product analytics dashboard. A leading indicator, the share of new accounts that attend the kickoff call in week one, is added so the team can see movement within weeks.

## A counter measure catches a side effect

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: a support team is measured on median first-response time, and its method is "respond to every ticket within four business hours."

**Walkthrough:**

At the first quarterly review, the response-time measure is green, but the team notices more tickets being reopened. Quick first replies are closing tickets before the problem is actually solved. The team adds a counter measure, the number of tickets reopened within a week, with a baseline taken from the last month. The next review looks at both numbers together, and the team changes its practice so the first reply confirms the problem instead of closing the ticket.

## Team measures that do not add up

**Scenario:**

Illustrative scenario: the company V2MOM targets 200 new mid-sized customers for the year. Three regional sales teams each write their own V2MOM with customer targets of 50, 50 and 60.

**Walkthrough:**

During the roll-up check, the sales director adds the team targets and gets 160, which leaves 40 customers with no owner. Rather than quietly raising each team's number, the director raises the gap with the company V2MOM owner. The result is a new partner-channel method with its own target of 40, owned by the partnerships lead, so the team targets now sum to the company target.
